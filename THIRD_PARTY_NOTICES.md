# Research attribution

This is an independent generator, not a fork or renamed boilerplate. Architectural research references:

- [vahiiiid/go-rest-api-boilerplate](https://github.com/vahiiiid/go-rest-api-boilerplate): domain boundaries, tested handlers, migrations and health checks.
- [codoworks/go-boilerplate](https://github.com/codoworks/go-boilerplate): selectable infrastructure concepts and operational CLI discoverability.

No reference source or template blocks were copied. Both local licenses are MIT. Their original notices are reproduced below for attribution. Application dependencies retain their own upstream licenses and are resolved by Go modules; this document does not relicense them.
